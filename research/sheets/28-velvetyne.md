## 1. What it is
A collective that publishes experimental and display typefaces by many designers, all free and libre. Examples: Ouroboros, Basteleur, Karrik, Mourier, Terminal Grotesque, Sligoski, Lack, Avara, Fengardo Neue.
## 2. How it works
Free download per font, with an optional donation. Sources are often on GitLab/GitHub.
## 3. Rules
- [type] OFL permits commercial use, self-hosting, and Elementor Custom Fonts upload. Keep OFL.txt with the files and don't sell the font on its own.
- [type] Use for display only (hero, headings, logos-in-type). Many lack full weight ranges, latin-ext coverage or good hinting, so pair them with a sturdy text face.
- [qa] Check glyph coverage for the client's language (e.g. Dutch ij, German ß, diacritics) before choosing a face.
