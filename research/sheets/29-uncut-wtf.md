# 29 — uncut.wtf
https://uncut.wtf · Website (curated free-font catalogue) · Licences vary per font ("All fonts copyright of their respective creators") · Stars n/a · read 2026-11-18 (homepage OK)

## 1. What it is
A catalogue of 163 "somewhat contemporary" typefaces marked free for commercial use: 66 sans, 27 serif, 23 mono, 47 display. It takes submissions and is set in its own Uncut Sans.
## 2. How it works
Grid or list browsing by category. Each entry links to the creator's download, so the licence comes from the creator, not uncut.
## 3. Rules
- [type] "Free for commercial use" is uncut's label, not a licence. Open each font's licence (OFL, CC BY, custom EULA) and record it before use. Custom EULAs may forbid webfont use, modification or logos.
- [wp] Ship only fonts whose licence explicitly allows web embedding or self-hosting. Keep the licence file next to the uploaded WOFF2 in Elementor Custom Fonts.

n/a. It's a source of non-default contemporary faces.

Take: a discovery source for the shortlist, but only add a font after its licence is verified and recorded, and prefer OFL entries. Skip: fonts with vague "free" terms or no licence file.
