# 30 — Typewolf
https://www.typewolf.com · Website (typography inspiration/reference by Jeremiah Shoaf) · Site content proprietary; fonts shown are mostly commercial · Stars n/a · read 2026-11-18 (homepage OK)

## 1. What it is
An independent type resource: Site of the Day (real sites with their fonts named), ranked font lists (popular, alternatives to Helvetica/Futura, free font lists), lookbooks, guides, and a paid Flawless Typography Checklist course ($399).
## 2. How it works
Curated editorial lists and screenshots. Each feature names the fonts and links to the foundries.
## 3. Rules
- [type] Use it as a reference for pairing logic and mood, not as a font source. Most featured fonts (e.g. Apercu, GT, Klim) are paid and need a web licence per client domain.
- [type] When a paid pick fits, use Typewolf's "free alternatives" lists or our OFL shortlist to find an open substitute. Only buy licences if the client pays for them.
- [brand] Find real sites in or near the niche and note their pairing pattern (serif + grotesk, mono accents) to avoid same-y outputs.
## 4. AI tells / anti-patterns it bans
n/a here. Its trend lists can also flag fonts that are becoming clichés.
## 5. Data, assets and scripts it ships
None. Editorial content only, no API.
## 6. Area
design / brand (inspiration)
## 7. Take / skip
Take: pairing patterns and the free-alternative mapping as input for the per-niche shortlist. Skip: copying screenshots or shipping paid fonts without a licence.
