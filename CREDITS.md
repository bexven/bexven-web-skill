## Notes on licences that matter in practice
- **WordPress/agent-skills (GPL-2.0-or-later):** no text copied; rules re-expressed in our own words.
- **Practical Typography (© Matthew Butterick) and Laws of UX:** all rights reserved; principles paraphrased only. Consider buying Practical Typography.
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts / Velvetyne are OFL (may be bundled); Fontshare is the ITF Free Font License (commercial use OK, no redistribution — never commit the files to public repos).
- **GSAP:** Webflow 'Standard No Charge' licence — free on client sites; do not build a competing visual animation builder.
- **Stock photos (Unsplash, Pexels):** free licences with restrictions (no implied endorsement, no identifiable people in sensitive contexts). Used as flagged placeholders only.
- **Galleries (Awwwards, Recent, SiteInspire, Land-book, Lapa Ninja, httpster, Minimal Gallery):** inspiration only; featured sites belong to their owners; no scraping.
- **Real-brand DESIGN.md files (awesome-design-md):** structure only; brand identities are not reused.
